The booksFind a duet🎲 Surprise us

1173 duets for one piano, four hands, in 23 books. Every piece comes in four editions: facing pages for printing (Secondo on the left, Primo on the right, lines and bar numbers matching), a printed full score, and two 16:10 screen editions for a laptop or tablet on the music stand. Dots rate each seat from 1 (beginner) to 5 (late intermediate); chord symbols are printed over both parts. ▶ practice opens a piece on the music stand with a player that can sound either part while you play the other.
