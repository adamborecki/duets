# Hymns for Organ & Piano

*Two instruments, two players*

The organ plays each hymn as it stands in the hymnal, with pedals; the piano adds the tune in octaves over flowing broken chords. Hymnal keys are kept. In the facing-pages edition the organ part is on the left-hand pages and the piano part on the right.

**24 duets.** Pick the edition that suits how you play:
